Twenty-four first-frame SVGs are included. Changes: SVG view boxes are cropped to square artwork bounds with padding, and the application scales them and uses a CSS monochrome filter on neutral backgrounds. The drawn paths are unchanged. Modified artwork remains CC BY-SA 4.0. No animation or reconstructed intermediate poses are included. Per-frame upstream attribution and original paths are preserved in `provenance.json`. Workout Guide credits 76 first poses as adaptations of Everkinetic; per-frame records are retained as provided by that source rather than assuming a specific original author where no further provenance is supplied.

These are list thumbnails that identify each exercise; they are not demonstrations of equipment setup or safe technique. Some show the closest catalogue movement rather than your exact setup (for example, a machine T-bar row for the landmine row, a two-hand cable extension for the one-arm version, and a bar for the fixed-handle knee raise). The paused bench and paused squat reuse the bench-press and squat drawings.
